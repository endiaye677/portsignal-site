SMS Consent

Pantry Route Delivery Notifications · Effective October 6, 2026

Pantry Route Delivery Notifications are text messages about home food deliveries from the Mosque Foundation Food Pantry in Bridgeview, IL. The program is operated by PorSignal LLC on behalf of the pantry.

How households opt in

Households opt in when they register for home delivery at the pantry, either on the pantry’s registration form, by agreeing to its text-message line, or verbally, when pantry staff read this exact script:

“Would you like to receive text messages about your food deliveries — a heads-up on delivery day and a message when the driver is on the way? You can reply STOP at any time to opt out.”

Staff record the household’s answer in Pantry Route. Only households that agree receive texts. Consent is never assumed or pre-selected, and the mobile number is used only for delivery messages.

Agreeing to texts is not a condition of receiving food. Households receive the same food and deliveries whether or not they choose to get text messages.

What messages you receive

Only messages about your own deliveries — never marketing. Messages are sent in the household’s preferred language (English, Spanish, or Arabic). For example:

  • Delivery-day reminder: Mosque Foundation Food Pantry: Your food delivery is scheduled for today. We'll text you when your driver is on the way.
  • Driver on the way: Mosque Foundation Food Pantry: Your driver is on the way now.
  • Occasionally, a notice if a delivery was missed or canceled, so you can arrange another time.

How often

Message frequency depends on your deliveries: typically 1–2 messages per delivery day, occasionally one more if a delivery is missed or canceled.

Rates, opting out, and help

  • Msg & data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time; you’ll receive no further texts. You can also tell your pantry coordinator.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email hadji@portsignal.org.

Privacy

Mobile numbers and SMS consent are never shared with or sold to third parties for marketing. See the Pantry Route privacy policy and the SMS terms.
